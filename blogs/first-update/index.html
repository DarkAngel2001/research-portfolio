Welcome to my blog. This environment tracks my ongoing cyber-infrastructure research updates, personal projects, and technical case studies.

I decided to build this space primarily because I needed a way to get all of these ideas out of my head. I absolutely love to study, dig into systems architecture, and uncover new technical vectors. However, up until now, I haven’t really had a dedicated, professional outlet to just rant (for lack of a better word) about my old projects, things I built purely for fun, or even the research proposals and assignments that didn't quite make it through administrative barriers.

That is exactly why this site exists. It is an unfiltered, structured outlet for my technical thoughts. If anyone out there wants to follow along, listen, or learn from my deep-dives, you are more than welcome.

To be completely transparent: please don't expect structured weekly updates or rigid posting schedules! I am treating this as a fluid, personal repository where I can showcase my portfolio, explore random architectural ideas, and document my engineering experiments without the weight of complete judgment.

From analyzing cross-platform entertainment streaming datasets in Python, to threat modeling national transit infrastructure, breaking down operating system kernels, or engineering privacy-preserving RAG pipelines for public-sector LLMs—this is my playground.

Please enjoy exploring the code, blueprints, and write-ups. If you ever want more information on a specific assessment, model, or script I’ve posted, feel free to email me!
